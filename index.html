"Investigating explainable NLP, multimodal reasoning, and language technologies for low-resource Indian and Dravidian languages."

I am an Assistant Professor at the Center for Artificial Intelligence Research (C-FAIR), Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore, India.

I received my Doctorate of Philosophy (Ph.D.) from the Department of Information Technology at National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK Surathkal). My PhD thesis focused on "An Explainable Textual and Multimodal Disinformation Detection Leveraging Large Language Models" under the guidance of Dr. Anand Kumar M.

My current research focuses on:

  • Large Language Models (LLMs) & XAI: Fine-tuning LLMs, windowed attention mechanisms, and explainable AI techniques for fake news detection.
  • Multimodal Disinformation & Misinformation: Cross-modal news analysis combining text and image reasoning.
  • Dravidian NLP: Building tools and benchmark models for code-mixed, low-resource languages (Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada).
  • Social Media Analytics: Toxic span identification, hope speech detection, and mental health indicator mining.
Looking for Collaborations? Open to joint research, PhD mentorship, and grants in AI, NLP, and Disinformation.

Research Thrust Areas

Overview of active projects and research trajectories at C-FAIR.

Explainable Disinformation Detection

Designing windowed transformer models and LLM reasoning pipelines (such as LeDoFAN) capable of handling long-form fake news documents while providing word-level and sentence-level explanations.

Low-Resource Dravidian Languages

Developing language processing models for code-mixed Dravidian scripts (Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada). Applications include toxic span detection, hope speech classification, and offensive content moderation.

Multimodal Disinformation Reasoning

Fusing visual evidence with natural language reasoning to uncover miscaptioned images, manipulated social media memes, and cross-modal discrepancies in news posts.

Class Imbalance & Overlapping Vocabulary

Investigating mathematical strategies for highly imbalanced corpora, including overlapping word removal, loss reweighting (Focal Loss), and SMOTE sampling in deep networks.
